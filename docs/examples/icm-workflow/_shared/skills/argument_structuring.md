# Skill — Argument structuring

## When to load
Stage 3, before writing `outline.md`.

## Structural principle
The outline is a **skeleton of claims**, not a skeleton of topics. Each section header should answer "what does the reader now know?" rather than "what is this section about?".

## Standard shape for an explainer
1. **Hook.** State the stakes concretely. Name a specific reader problem.
2. **Definition / framing.** The minimum vocabulary needed to follow.
3. **Mechanism.** How the thing works, at the level of detail the audience needs.
4. **Application / examples.** Where the mechanism meets reality.
5. **Risks, limits, open questions.** Honesty about what is unresolved.
6. **Action.** What the reader should do differently on Monday.

## Section budgeting
Distribute the target word count across sections before writing prose. A section over 25 % of the budget is usually two sections in disguise.

## Coverage test
Every research question in the scope brief must map to at least one section. Every section must map back to at least one RQ. Print the mapping in `handoff.md`.
